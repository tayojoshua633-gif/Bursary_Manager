## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: Do I need internet to use Bursary Manager?**
A: No! The app works 100% offline in standalone mode. Internet is only needed for Google Drive backup (optional) or client-server mode over local network.

**Q: Can multiple staff use it at the same time?**
A: Yes! Set up one computer as server, others as clients. Multiple staff can work simultaneously with role-based permissions.

**Q: What happens if my computer crashes?**
A: Your database is automatically backed up to Google Drive (if enabled). You can also manually backup anytime. Simply install on a new computer and restore your backup.

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes! Passwords are encrypted with bcrypt. Data stays on your devices. Google Drive backup uses your personal account. License prevents unauthorized access.
